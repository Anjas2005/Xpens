## Brand & Style
The design system combines the trust, clarity, and structural precision of tier-one modern consumer fintech with the cultural warmth and energy of modern India. Inspired by cutting-edge digital banking leaders, the aesthetic fuses clean geometric typography with a tactile, sunlit palette rooted in saffron, turmeric, and deep ivory.

The design movement is **Modern Warm Neomorphism with Tactile Precision**. It rejects sterile, cold-blue corporate fintech tropes in favor of an inviting, human, yet deeply secure visual environment. The interface feels premium, snappy, and grounded—conveying prosperity, fiscal transparency, and frictionless digital transactions across varied devices and ambient lighting conditions.

## Colors
The color architecture is built around cultural signifiers re-engineered for strict digital contrast and utility:

- **Primary Brand (`#E8751A`)**: Deep Saffron Orange drives high-intent touchpoints, key calls-to-action, active bottom-navigation tabs, and signature reward elements.
- **Primary Dark (`#C95D0C`)**: Burnt Orange serves as the focused hover/press state for primary actions and high-emphasis focal strokes.
- **Secondary (`#16834B`)**: Indian Green denotes fiscal growth, incoming transfers, positive returns, investments, and UPI verification confirmation states.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#F4B942`)**: Turmeric Yellow acts as a luminous micro-accent for badges, reward coin indicators, and secondary visual tags.
- **Canvas & Surfaces**: Base canvas utilizes Warm Ivory (`#FFFDF8`) to eliminate harsh sterile glares, while interactive cards, sheets, and drawers rest on pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Dividers & Structural Borders**: Soft Beige (`#E9E3D8`) maintains separation without visual noise.
- **Typography Spectrum**: Deep Charcoal (`#202124`) provides primary legibility; Warm Gray (`#6B6B6B`) handles metadata, transaction stamps, and input placeholders.
- **Semantic Feedback**: Expense/Debit activity uses sharp Carmine (`#D64545`), while Income/Credit maintains parity with Indian Green (`#16834B`).

## Typography
Manrope delivers a balance of modern geometric structure and friendly humanist open counters. Numerals are calibrated for tabular and financial clarity—critical for displaying rupee balances (₹), transaction amounts, and percentage yields. 

Hierarchy is established via distinct weight jumps (`800` for bold portfolio snapshots and hero values, stepping down to `500` and `400` for clean legibility across dense banking statements and form configurations). Letter-spacing tightens slightly on larger displays to retain density and punch, relaxing into neutral tracking for operational body text.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to an 8pt base grid with a 4pt sub-grid for micro-adjustments. 

- **Mobile (< 600px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` outer canvas margin and `1rem` gutters. Crucial actions, UPI quick-actions, and card stacks span the full width to facilitate thumb accessibility.
- **Tablet (600px – 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and gutters. Layout splits account balances and transaction ledgers side by side.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout capped at an architectural max-width of `1200px` centered within a `2.5rem` canvas margin, utilizing `1.5rem` gutters.

Spacing enforces breathing room between high-density financial data modules. Component paddings consistently utilize `space-md` for standard cells and `space-lg` for primary summary tiles.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth avoids heavy generic drop shadows, favoring warm ambient diffusion tinted with subtle golden and charcoal undertones:

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: Warm Ivory (`#FFFDF8`) ground without shadow.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Modules, List Items)**: White (`#FFFFFF`) surface combined with a dual-layer shadow: `0 2px 4px rgba(32, 33, 36, 0.02), 0 8px 16px rgba(232, 117, 26, 0.04)` wrapped in a 1px perimeter border of Soft Beige (`#E9E3D8`).
- **Level 2 (Active Sheets, Popovers, Dropdowns)**: White surface floating over `0 12px 28px rgba(32, 33, 36, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Bottom Drawers, Critical Alerts)**: Pure surface lifted by `0 20px 48px rgba(32, 33, 36, 0.14)` over a warm tinted backdrop blur (`rgba(32, 33, 36, 0.4)` with 8px blur).
- **Interactive State Depth**: Primary saffron buttons utilize a soft glow in pressed states: `0 4px 14px rgba(232, 117, 26, 0.35)`.

## Shapes
The design system operates at level `2` (Rounded). This geometric language echoes modern consumer app conventions, balancing soft approachability with systematic discipline:

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges)**: 0.5rem (8px) radius.
- **Content Cards, Modal dialogs, Summary Tiles (`rounded-lg`)**: 1rem (16px) radius.
- **Bottom Drawers, Feature Promo Hero Banners (`rounded-xl`)**: 1.5rem (24px) radius.
- **Avatars, Status Pills, Micro Chips**: Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Deep Saffron (`#E8751A`) with white typography (`label-lg`), height 48px (mobile) or 44px (desktop), 8px border radius. Hover/active shifts to Burnt Orange (`#C95D0C`).
- **Secondary / Success**: Subtle Indian Green tint background (`rgba(22, 131, 75, 0.08)`), solid `#16834B` text, 1px border of `rgba(22, 131, 75, 0.2)`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Transparent background, Deep Charcoal (`#202124`) text with 1px border in Soft Beige (`#E9E3D8`).

### Input Fields & Amount Selectors
- **Text Inputs**: White container, 48px height, 8px radius, bordered by Soft Beige (`#E9E3D8`). Active focus shifts border to `#E8751A` with a 2px outer glow (`rgba(232, 117, 26, 0.15)`).
- **Rupee Transaction Inputs**: Frameless, displaying `currency-display` scale. Pre-fixed with a static saffron or charcoal ₹ glyph, auto-resizing as digits are entered.

### Cards & Account Tiles
- **Surface**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with 16px radius, enclosed by a Soft Beige stroke.
- **Transaction Cards**: Inner padding of 16px, incorporating circular merchant icon containers (40px, rounded-full, tinted background), flexed against a bold balance alignment (`body-lg` + semantic color for credit/debit).

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Filter Chips**: Height 32px, pill-shaped (`9999px`), Soft Beige border, `#FFFDF8` background.
- **Active Selection**: Solid Deep Charcoal background with crisp white text, or Deep Saffron border with warm saffron tint background (`rgba(232, 117, 26, 0.1)`).

### UPI & Verification Elements
- **VPA / Phone Handle Badges**: Compact capsule featuring an Indian Green checkmark badge paired with Turmeric Yellow accent highlights for verified accounts.
- **Transaction State Trackers**: Multi-step vertical or horizontal segmented progress bar using Indian Green for completed steps, Saffron for active processing, and Soft Beige for upcoming legs.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Control Indicators**: 20px box/circle with a 1.5px Soft Beige border. Checked state transitions to a solid Deep Saffron fill with an inverted white checkmark or inner dot.
